Methodology

Key takeaways

  • Flags are generated by explicit rules, not by a language model. A move is flagged when its one-day return exceeds 2.0 standard deviations of its trailing 252-session distribution.
  • Every threshold is set in config.yaml.

Today's calendar

  • Release dates come from FRED, filtered to a whitelist of major US releases. Release IDs are resolved by name rather than hardcoded.
  • FRED does not publish release times. Times shown are typical ET publication times and are not guaranteed.
  • FOMC dates are maintained in config.yaml from federalreserve.gov and must be updated each year.
  • Notable earnings are drawn from a curated watchlist of companies above roughly $10bn market capitalisation.

Overnight & futures

  • Equity futures are shown against the prior cash index close, which is what a pre-open futures move is normally quoted against.
  • Pre-open quotes come from Yahoo Finance. Polygon's free tier has no snapshot endpoint, so this is a documented exception to the project's rule that intraday data comes from Polygon.
  • Quotes are indicative and may be delayed.

Equities

  • Returns are simple percentage changes over 5, 21, 63 and 252 trading days; YTD is measured from the final close of the previous calendar year.
  • Volatility is the annualised standard deviation of daily returns over 20 sessions.
  • The z-score compares the latest one-day return with its trailing 252-session distribution.
  • Prices are dividend-adjusted, so multi-month returns are total returns.

Rates & the Fed

  • Yields are FRED constant-maturity series, which are published with a one- to two-day lag; each figure carries its observation date.
  • Changes are in basis points. Curve snapshots use the last observation on or before the target date, since the series skip weekends.
  • Bull/bear describes the direction of yields; steepener/flattener the change in the 10-minus-2 spread. Moves below 2bp are treated as unchanged.

Credit

  • Option-adjusted spreads over Treasuries, in percentage points; changes are in basis points.
  • Percentiles and z-scores use up to 252 sessions, clamped to available history (shortest here: 3.0 years).

Macro dashboard

  • Transforms are declared per series in config.yaml: year-on-year for price indices, a three-month average change for payrolls, and a four-week average for jobless claims.
  • Year-on-year is measured from the underlying level. Series that are themselves rates are shown in percentage points (pp), and indices that cross zero in index points (pts), because a percentage change of a near-zero base is meaningless.
  • FRED dates an observation at the start of its period, so a current monthly series can legitimately look six weeks old. The stale badge therefore fires on a missed scheduled release, not on age.
  • ISM PMI data is not available on FRED. The regional Federal Reserve manufacturing surveys are shown as proxies and labelled as such.

FX, commodities & crypto

  • Futures prices are continuous front-month series. Returns spanning a contract roll are suppressed rather than shown, because the price step at a roll is an artefact of the series, not a market move.
  • The copper/gold ratio is a common proxy for growth expectations; plotting it against the 10-year yield compares it with what the bond market is pricing.

Cross-asset & regime

  • Correlations use 60 sessions of daily changes. Credit spreads are differenced in basis points rather than percentage-changed, which would be meaningless near zero.
  • Volatility percentiles rank the current 20-day realised volatility within its own trailing 252 sessions.
  • The regime composite z-scores each component against its own trailing 504 sessions, applies the configured sign and weight, and re-normalises over whichever components have data.
  • The composite is a descriptive summary of conditions relative to recent history. It is not a forecast, a recommendation, or a trading signal.

Headlines

  • Headlines only — no article text is reproduced. Links open the original.
  • Near-duplicate headlines from syndicated wire stories are collapsed.
